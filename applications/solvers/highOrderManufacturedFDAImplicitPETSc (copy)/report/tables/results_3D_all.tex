\paragraph{3D comments and conclusions.}
The three nonlinear strategies give indistinguishable convergence orders in the 3D MMS data when the same spatial, temporal, and mass-matrix settings are used. For the structured hexa mesh, the \code{p3}/\code{p2} and \code{p3}/\code{p3} configurations with $\Delta t=10^{-3}$ recover approximately fourth-order reconstructed diagnostics, while the cell-centred voltage remains slightly below fourth order over $N=10$--$30$. The unstructured simplex mesh is more sensitive to $\alpha$ and to the available mesh range, but the same high-order configurations remain the clearest verification cases. Runtime and RSS columns should be read as accumulated sweep costs, not single-run costs.
