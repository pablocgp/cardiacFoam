\section{Governing Equation}
The solver advances the transient scalar diffusion problem
\begin{equation}
\rho C_p\frac{\partial T}{\partial t} = \nabla\cdot(\alpha\nabla T)+Q.
\end{equation}
For \code{example5}, the residual uses the same FDA-like manufactured source as the explicit heat solver,
\begin{equation}
R_K = L_h(T)_K - Q_K,
\end{equation}
where $Q$ is evaluated from an exact manufactured ionic-current expression but no ODE variables are solved.
\section{Manufactured Example5}
The exact field is
\begin{equation}
T^\star(\mathbf{x},t)=A\sqrt{1+t}F(\mathbf{x}).
\end{equation}
In 2D,
\begin{align}
F(x,y)&=\cos(\pi x)\cos(2\pi y), & G(x,y)&=1+xy^2.
\end{align}
The source uses
\begin{align}
Q^\star &= A I_{\mathrm{ion}}^\star,\\
I_{\mathrm{ion}}^\star &= -\frac12\left(u_1^\star+u_3^\star-T^\star\right)(u_2^\star)^2\left(T^\star-u_3^\star\right)+\beta_T\left(T^\star-u_3^\star\right),\\
u_1^\star&=(1+t)G+T^\star, & u_2^\star&=\frac{1}{(1+t)\sqrt{G}}, & u_3^\star&=0.
\end{align}
The auxiliary $u_i^\star$ enter only through the manufactured source.
\section{Spatial Discretization}
For \code{useHighOrder\_T=false}, the solver uses the standard OpenFOAM cell-centred finite-volume Laplacian. For \code{useHighOrder\_T=true}, the diffusion operator is assembled from LRE reconstructions into a high-order stiffness matrix $K$ and mass matrix $M$. In semi-discrete notation,
\begin{equation}
M\frac{dT}{dt} = K T + M Q,
\end{equation}
with the sign of $Q$ adjusted for \code{example5} in the code. The options \code{p1}, \code{p2}, and \code{p3} select first-, second-, and third-order reconstruction terms, while \code{NO} uses the standard second-order finite-volume operator.
\section{Mass Matrices}
The high-order solve can use a lumped or consistent mass matrix. The lumped option reduces the mass to diagonal cell-volume weights. The consistent option integrates the reconstruction basis over cell quadrature points, which couples stencil coefficients through the mass matrix. For standard \code{NO} runs, only the lumped finite-volume mass is used in the current results.
\section{Boundary Conditions}
For the high-order flux/operator, \code{zeroGradient} and \code{empty} patches are treated as no-flux boundaries,
\begin{equation}
\int_{\partial K\cap\Gamma_N} \mathbf{n}\cdot\alpha\nabla T\,dS=0.
\end{equation}
Fixed-value patches keep their boundary contribution in the high-order matrix/source assembly.
\section{Implicit Time Integration}
The solver uses a theta method,
\begin{equation}
\theta=1\quad\text{for backward Euler},\qquad \theta=\frac12\quad\text{for Crank--Nicolson}.
\end{equation}
A compact form of the linear heat solve is
\begin{equation}
\left(\frac{M}{\Delta t}-\theta K\right)T^{n+1}
=\left(\frac{M}{\Delta t}+(1-\theta)K\right)T^n
+\theta M Q^{n+1}+(1-\theta)M Q^n,
\end{equation}
with the appropriate source sign for \code{example5}.
\section{Error Measures and Rates}
The solver writes cell-centred errors $L_1$, $L_2$, $L_\infty$ and reconstructed quadrature errors $G_1$, $G_2$, $G_\infty$. The tables below recompute the $L_2$ and $G_2$ log--log rates directly from the \path{errors_vs_N} files. The expected spatial orders are approximately $2$ for \code{NO} and \code{p1}, $3$ for \code{p2}, and $4$ for \code{p3}.
